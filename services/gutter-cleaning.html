Gutter Cleaning

What’s Included

Saskatoon's elm-lined streets are beautiful — until autumn leaves back up your eavestroughs. Our gutter cleaning service in Saskatoon clears debris from gutters and downspouts by hand, then checks that water is flowing freely before we leave.

Clogged gutters are one of the most common (and most preventable) causes of basement leaks and siding damage in older Saskatoon neighbourhoods. A once or twice yearly clean-out is cheap insurance against a much bigger repair bill.

  • Full removal of leaves, needles, and debris by hand
  • Downspout flow test on every downspout
  • Exterior gutter face wiped down
  • Roof edge and valley debris check
  • Photos of before/after on request
