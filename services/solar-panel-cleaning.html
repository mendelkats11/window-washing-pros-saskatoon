Solar Panel Cleaning

What’s Included

A layer of dust or grime can measurably cut a solar array's output — and with Saskatoon's dry summers and prairie wind, panels collect buildup fast. Our solar panel cleaning service uses soft, non-abrasive brushes and deionized water so panels come away spot-free with no scratching.

We service roof-mounted and ground-mount arrays on homes and small commercial buildings, and can pair panel cleaning with a gutter or exterior window visit to save you a trip charge.

  • Soft-brush, no-scratch cleaning process
  • Deionized water for a spot-free finish
  • Roof-mounted & ground-mount arrays
  • Visual inspection for debris or nesting
  • Optional pairing with gutter cleaning
