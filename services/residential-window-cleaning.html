Residential Windows

What’s Included

If you're searching for reliable residential window cleaning in Saskatoon, you've found your crew. We clean homes across every neighbourhood in the city — from Nutana and Varsity View to Stonebridge and Rosewood — using a squeegee-and-purified-water process that leaves glass streak-free without any harsh chemical residue on your sills or landscaping.

Most homeowners book us for a seasonal refresh (spring and fall are our busiest times) but we also offer one-time cleans for move-ins, open houses, or after a Saskatoon dust storm rolls through. Every visit includes a full interior and exterior wash, with track and screen detailing available as an add-on.

  • Interior & exterior glass, washed streak-free
  • Window sills and frames wiped down
  • Screens removed, rinsed, and dried
  • Tracks vacuumed and scrubbed free of debris
  • Cobwebs cleared from window wells and eaves
