Post-Construction Cleanup

What’s Included

Whether it's a new build in Brighton or a window replacement on an older Saskatoon character home, freshly installed glass needs more than a quick wipe. Our post-construction cleaning service removes manufacturer stickers, protective film, paint overspray, and fine construction dust safely, without scratching new glass.

We work directly with homeowners, contractors, and builders to schedule the final clean once other trades are finished, so your new windows are ready to show the moment you walk through the door.

  • Manufacturer sticker & label removal
  • Protective film stripped and disposed of
  • Paint overspray and adhesive residue removal
  • Fine construction dust cleaned from sills and tracks
  • Final streak-free polish, interior & exterior
