Pressure & Soft Washing

What’s Included

Winter road grit and salt take a toll on Saskatoon driveways and siding. Our pressure washing service in Saskatoon lifts ground-in dirt, salt stains, and algae from concrete, brick, and wood without damaging the surface underneath.

For softer surfaces like siding, stucco, and painted decks, we switch to a lower-pressure soft-wash process with a cleaning solution that breaks down grime before a gentle rinse — the safer choice for anything that isn't solid concrete.

  • Driveways, walkways, and patios
  • Vinyl, wood, and stucco siding (soft wash)
  • Decks and fences
  • Salt and grit removal from concrete
  • Surface-appropriate pressure and technique
