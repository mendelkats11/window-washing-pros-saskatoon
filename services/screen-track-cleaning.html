Screen & Track Detailing

What’s Included

Window tracks quietly collect years of dust, dead insects, and grit that a quick wipe never reaches. Our screen and track cleaning add-on is popular with Saskatoon homeowners who notice their windows have gotten stiff to open or their screens have gone grey.

We remove each screen, rinse and air-dry it separately, then vacuum and hand-scrub the tracks before your windows go back together — usually adding only a few minutes per window to a standard cleaning visit.

  • Screens removed, rinsed, and air-dried
  • Tracks vacuumed to remove loose debris
  • Tracks hand-scrubbed and wiped clean
  • Sliding mechanism check for smoother operation
  • Screens reinstalled and inspected for tears
