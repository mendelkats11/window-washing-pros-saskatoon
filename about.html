Our Story

A Local Crew That Cares About the Details

Window Washing Pros of Saskatoon was built on a simple idea: exterior cleaning should be dependable, thorough, and done by people who actually live here. What began as a small residential window-cleaning outfit has grown into a full-service exterior care company — without losing the personal touch that got us started.

Today our crews handle everything from single-storey bungalows to multi-unit commercial buildings, using the same checklist-driven process and streak-free standard on every single job.
